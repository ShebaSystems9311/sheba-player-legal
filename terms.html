03 Description of the Application

Sheba Player is a desktop media player for Microsoft Windows. It is designed to play audio and video files stored locally on your device. It includes tools for library management, audio equalisation, playlist creation, and visual display of audio content.

Sheba Player is a local application only. It does not stream content from the internet, does not connect to any online service, and does not require an account or subscription to use.
